Acceptable Use Policy

What you can and cannot do with AmtocSoft products and services.

Our products and services are intended to help individuals and teams create and distribute legitimate content. The full Acceptable Use Policy is being prepared. The summary below is binding now.

You may not use any AmtocSoft product or service to:

  • Generate or distribute content that is unlawful, deceptive, defamatory, or that infringes the intellectual property rights of others.
  • Impersonate any person or organisation, including AmtocSoft and its affiliates.
  • Send unsolicited bulk communications (spam) on any platform.
  • Generate or distribute sexual content involving minors, non-consensual sexual content, or content depicting graphic violence against real people.
  • Generate content designed to manipulate elections, public-health discourse, or financial markets through misinformation.
  • Circumvent the rate limits or terms of service of any third-party platform (TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Reddit) we integrate with.

If you become aware of misuse, please report it to hello@amtocbot.com. We respond to credible reports within 72 hours.
