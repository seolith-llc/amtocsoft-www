Refund Policy

When you can ask for your money back, and how to do it.

The detailed refund policy for AmtocSoft products is being prepared. The summary below reflects the policy we will apply consistently.

  • Digital downloads (the Kit, individual modules, the book): non-refundable once delivered, because the buyer has irreversible access to the content.
  • Delivery problems: if you cannot access a product you purchased due to a technical issue on our side, contact us within 7 days and we will resolve it or refund the purchase in full.
  • Services (audits, consulting calls): refundable if cancelled at least 24 hours before the scheduled session. After the session begins, no refund.
  • Subscription products: we do not currently offer subscription products. This section will be updated if that changes.

To start a refund request, email hello@amtocbot.com with your order reference.
